# App promo artwork

The banner background was independently generated with the built-in image_gen tool. No assets were cropped, traced, extracted or reused from the reference screenshot. The phone frame, mobile screen, store links and glass callouts are rendered by React and CSS. Existing promotional and phone-screen copy and both https://footygen.online links are preserved.

Delivery: `stadium-night.webp` (2170px wide, 148,968 bytes) and `stadium-night-mobile.webp` (1200px wide, 52,188 bytes). The full-resolution original remains in the tool's generated-images directory. These are decorative backgrounds, not screenshots of interactive UI.

At about 66 percent across and 73 percent down place a beautiful dark navy and silver football with realistic stitched pentagonal leather panels, illuminated with electric blue rim lighting, partially nestled among dark wet rugged rocks and tiny natural grass blades in the foreground. Rocks extend along the lower-right edge. Thin elegant cobalt and cyan energy light trails sweep from the lower left toward the football, curving upward into one glowing orbital arc around the right side, with a few very restrained blue sparks. The right-center around 78 percent across should leave space ABOVE the rocks for a smartphone that will be implemented separately in HTML. Close low camera angle at pitch level, tactile wet stone and grass, controlled deep blacks, luminous blue highlights, stadium bokeh and depth of field. The background should be luxurious and legible rather than busy. No smartphone, no device, no screen, no UI, no text, no lettering, no typography, no badges, no logos, no watermark, no border or frame. No people or players. Fill the full rectangular canvas; do not add rounded corners. Independently create this image; do not crop, trace, extract, or reuse a screenshot. Wide landscape aspect ratio approximately 3.15:1.
